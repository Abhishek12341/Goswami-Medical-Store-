A balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains and protein provides essential nutrients. Limit processed foods and excess sugar. Eating at regular times supports metabolism and long-term wellness.

Exercise Daily - Read More

At least 30 minutes of moderate activity most days can improve heart health, mood and stamina. Walking, cycling or yoga are simple options. Consistency matters more than intensity for beginners.
